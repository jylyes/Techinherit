ELASTOMERS

TPE
Thermoplastic Elastomer

TPE is a versatile material combining the elastic properties of rubber with the processability of thermoplastics. It offers excellent flexibility over a wide temperature range, good chemical resistance, and soft-touch feel. TPE can be injection molded, extruded, and overmolded, making it ideal for grips, seals, automotive interiors, and medical devices.
