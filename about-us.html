About Techinherit

From concept development to mass production, we help global customers turn ideas into reliable plastic products. With strong engineering capabilities and a complete industrial chain, we deliver efficient one-stop solutions.

ABOUT US

Your Trusted Plastic
Manufacturing Expert

Techinherit specializes in the development and manufacturing of customized plastic products. We possess a complete one-stop industrial chain, encompassing plastic product design, material research and development, mold development, injection molding, and finished product assembly. Leveraging our strong engineering capabilities, we are deeply involved in our clients' product development, helping them reduce development risks, shorten delivery cycles, and achieve stable mass production with high quality, high efficiency, and high cost-effectiveness. The company is committed to technology development, quality assurance, and specialized focus on plastics, providing clients with the highest quality and most professional complete plastic technology solutions.
