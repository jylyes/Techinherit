ENGINEERING PLASTIC

PMMA (Acrylic)
Polymethyl Methacrylate

PMMA is an amorphous thermoplastic known for its exceptional optical clarity, high UV and weather resistance, and excellent surface finish. With light transmittance up to 92%, it is a lightweight alternative to glass. PMMA also offers good scratch resistance, stiffness, and weatherability, making it ideal for automotive lighting, signage, and display applications.
