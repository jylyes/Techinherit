OUR INJECTION MOLDING PROCESS

Efficient Process. Consistent Quality.

We follow a standardized injection molding process to ensure every part is produced with precision, reliability, and efficiency.

01

Material Preparation

High-quality Plastic Raw Materials are dried and prepared to ensure optimal molding performance.

02

Mold Setup

The mold is installed and precisely set up with optimized process parameters.

03

Injection Molding

Molten plastic is injected into the mold under high pressure and tightly controlled conditions.

04

Cooling & Solidification

Parts are cooled in the mold to solidify and ensure dimensional stability and consistency.

05

Part Ejection

Finished parts are ejected automatically to ensure efficiency and prevent damage.

06

Inspection & Packaging

Each part is inspected for quality and then packed carefully for delivery.
