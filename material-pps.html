HIGH-PERFORMANCE PLASTIC

PPS
Polyphenylene Sulfide

PPS is a high-performance semi-crystalline engineering plastic offering exceptional thermal stability (continuous use up to 220°C), inherent flame retardancy (UL94 V-0 without additives), and outstanding chemical resistance against virtually all organic solvents. It exhibits excellent dimensional stability, high stiffness, and low creep.
