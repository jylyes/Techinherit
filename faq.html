Q&A Center

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our plastic manufacturing services, process, and quality standards.

Home/Q&A Center
What is the typical lead time for mold development?

Standard mold development takes 25-40 days depending on complexity. We offer accelerated timelines for urgent projects. After mold approval, injection molding production typically starts within 7-15 days.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for injection molding?

Our MOQ varies by part size and material. For standard parts, MOQ starts at 1,000-5,000 pieces. For prototype or low-volume runs, we offer flexible arrangements. Contact us with your part details for a specific quote.

Do you offer product design and DFM analysis before tooling?

Yes. We provide comprehensive DFM (Design for Manufacturing) analysis at no charge before tooling begins. Our engineering team reviews your design for moldability, material suitability, and cost optimization opportunities.

What industries do you serve?

We serve a wide range of industries including new energy (EV components), AI hardware, consumer electronics, medical devices, office furniture, pet supplies, automotive, and industrial equipment. Our material and process capabilities support diverse applications.

What quality certifications does Techinherit hold?

Techinherit is ISO 9001:2015 certified for quality management. We also comply with ISO 13485 for medical device components, RoHS and REACH for material safety, and can provide material certification documents upon request.

How do you ensure quality during mass production?

We implement a multi-stage QC process: incoming material inspection, first article inspection (FAI), in-process SPC monitoring, and final outgoing inspection. Each batch includes dimensional reports, material certifications, and visual inspection records.

Do you offer global shipping and logistics support?

Yes. We ship worldwide via sea, air, or express. Our logistics team handles documentation, customs clearance, and door-to-door delivery. We have successfully shipped to 50+ countries across North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East.

Can you help with custom material formulation?

Absolutely. Our in-house R&D lab specializes in custom material compounding. Whether you need enhanced flame retardancy, UV resistance, impact modification, or conductive properties, we can develop a tailored formulation to meet your specific requirements.
