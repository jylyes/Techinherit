Waterproof Connector

High Sealing, High Insulation, Corrosion Resistant
Waterproof connector for new energy applications, requiring IP67/IP68 sealing, high insulation performance, corrosion resistance and long-term reliability in harsh environments.
Material
  • PA6 GF30% / CTI 600V
  • UV stabilized, weather resistant
  • High insulation performance
  • UL94 V-0 @0.8mm
Mold
  • Precision mold with slide core
  • Mold steel: S136
  • Key dimensions: ±0.03mm
  • Mold life: ≥1M cycles
Injection
  • Machine: 160T
  • Drying: 85°C / 4-6H
  • Mold temp: 110-120°C
  • Cavity pressure monitoring + SPC
Key Results
Waterproof Rating IP68
Precision ±0.05mm
Insulation Resistance >500MΩ
Yield Rate 99.6%
