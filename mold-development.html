OUR MOLD DEVELOPMENT SERVICES

Complete Mold Solutions Under One Roof

We manage every stage of the mold development process to deliver high-quality molds that meet your exact requirements.

Mold Design

DFM analysis, 3D modeling and mold flow simulation to optimize design for manufacturability.

CNC Machining

High-precision machining using advanced CNC equipment.

EDM Processing

Wire EDM and sinker EDM for complex geometries and high-precision details.

Mold Assembly

Expert assembly to ensure mold accuracy, functionality, and durability.

Mold Testing

Trial runs and part validation to ensure the mold meets all specifications.

Regular maintenance and support to extend mold life and performance.
