ENGINEERING PLASTIC

PBT
Polybutylene Terephthalate

PBT is a semi-crystalline engineering thermoplastic from the polyester family. It offers high strength, excellent electrical properties, good dimensional stability, and outstanding chemical resistance. PBT has fast crystallization rates, enabling shorter cycle times in injection molding, and is widely used in electrical/electronic and automotive applications.
