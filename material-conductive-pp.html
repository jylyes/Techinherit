CONDUCTIVE / ESD PLASTIC

Conductive PP
Conductive Polypropylene

Conductive PP is a cost-effective ESD-safe material combining polypropylene's excellent chemical resistance and low moisture absorption with permanent electrical conductivity. It provides reliable static dissipation at an economical price, ideal for high-volume ESD packaging, automation components, and industrial handling applications.
