Patient Monitor Enclosure

High Gloss, Chemical Resistant, Easy to Clean
The monitor enclosure requires high gloss, resistance to disinfectant wiping, dimensional stability, and no deformation over long-term use.
Material
  • PC+ABS Medical Grade
  • Flame retardant: UL94 V-0
  • Excellent chemical resistance
  • High gloss, dimensional stability
  • ISO 10993 compliant
Mold
  • High-gloss mirror mold
  • Mold steel: NAK80
  • Surface polish: SPI A2
  • Precision: ±0.02mm
  • Mold life: ≥800K cycles
Injection
  • High-gloss injection molding
  • Machine: 380T
  • Mold temp: 60-80°C
  • Injection pressure: 1200bar
  • Surface: High-gloss coating
Key Results
Surface Gloss >90GU
Precision ±0.08mm
Disinfectant Wipe >1000x
Medical Cert ISO 13485
