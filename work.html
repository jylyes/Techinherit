How We Prepare Raw Materials Before Injection Molding

Every production run starts with careful material preparation. Our team dries hygroscopic resins, mixes masterbatch additives, and verifies material specifications before loading into hoppers — ensuring consistent melt quality and stable molding parameters from the first shot.

Mold Setup & Parameter Calibration: Why It Matters

Proper mold installation and parameter calibration are critical to product quality. Our technicians follow a documented setup procedure — temperature profiling, clamping force adjustment, ejection timing calibration — to minimize startup scrap and ensure repeatable quality.

From Injection to Ejection: A Tour of Our Molding Workshop

Step inside our 30-machine injection workshop. From robotic part removal to conveyor belt sorting, every station is optimized for speed and precision. Real-time monitoring tracks pressure, temperature, and cycle time — alerting operators at the first sign of deviation.

Our Multi-Layer Quality Inspection System Explained

Quality is not a single checkpoint — it is embedded throughout the process. From incoming material melt flow testing to in-process SPC charts and final CMM dimensional validation, our three-tier inspection protocol catches defects before they reach the customer.

Quality InspectionMay 20, 2026

How We Test Plastic Parts: From Tensile Strength to Drop Resistance

Different products demand different testing protocols. Our lab performs tensile, impact, hardness, and thermal testing on every new material batch. For finished products, functional tests including assembly fit, drop test, and load bearing validation ensure real-world reliability.

Pre-Shipment Audit: The Final Gate Before Delivery

Before any order leaves our facility, it passes through a pre-shipment audit. Random sampling at AQL 1.5, visual defect inspection, dimensional spot checks, and packaging verification — all documented in a final inspection report shared with the customer.

How We Package Your Products for Safe Global Shipping

Custom foam inserts, humidity indicators, vacuum sealing, reinforced cartons — our packaging solutions are tailored to product fragility, shipping method, and destination climate. Every pallet is photographed and documented before dispatch.
