DFM & Cost Optimization

Before mold manufacturing, our engineers conduct comprehensive DFM analysis to identify potential risks and optimize product manufacturability.

  • Reduce production cost
  • Improve product quality
  • Shorten development cycle
  • Avoid mold modifications
  • Improve assembly efficiency

Plastic Material Engineering

We work with a wide range of engineering plastics: ABS, PC, PA66, PP, POM, TPU, PC+ABS.

  • Material recommendation
  • Performance analysis
  • Cost comparison
  • Environmental compliance
  • Durability optimization

Mold Flow Analysis

Using advanced mold flow simulation technology, we evaluate filling performance, cooling efficiency, shrinkage behavior, and potential defect risks before mold production.

  • Reduce trial costs
  • Improve molding stability
  • Minimize warpage
  • Shorten development time
